# PROJECT

## Product
Official website for Tile Mart, a tile and sanitary-products business serving Peshawar, Pakistan.

## Primary outcomes
1. Generate qualified WhatsApp enquiries.
2. Generate phone calls.
3. Increase showroom visits.
4. Build strong local-search visibility for tile and sanitary-product intent in Peshawar.
5. Present the business as established, trustworthy, modern, and product-focused.

## Audience
- Homeowners renovating or building
- Builders and contractors
- Architects and interior designers
- Developers
- Customers shopping for bathroom and sanitary products
- Local buyers comparing tile styles, sizes, finishes, and price ranges

## Product taxonomy
Design the site to support these categories, but publish only categories that Tile Mart verifies it sells:
- Floor tiles
- Wall tiles
- Bathroom tiles
- Kitchen tiles
- Porcelain tiles
- Ceramic tiles
- Outdoor tiles
- Large-format and slab products
- Wash basins
- WCs and toilets
- Faucets and mixers
- Showers
- Bathroom accessories
- Complete sanitary and bathroom sets

## Brand direction
Use the supplied Tile Mart logo as the identity reference. The website should feel architectural, premium, clean, tactile, and credible rather than flashy. Avoid generic construction-template aesthetics, fake luxury, excessive gradients, stock-photo clutter, and unsupported claims such as “#1” or “best in Pakistan.”

## Languages
V1: English-first, with content architecture prepared for future Urdu and Pashto support if requested.

## Conversion model
No full e-commerce checkout in V1 unless later requested. Products act as discovery and enquiry pages. Primary calls to action are WhatsApp, phone, directions, and product enquiries.

## Content integrity
Never invent product brands, prices, stock, sizes, guarantees, years in business, delivery promises, opening hours, or customer reviews. Use placeholders or omit a claim until confirmed.
