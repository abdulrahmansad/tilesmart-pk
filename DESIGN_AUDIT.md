# Design audit

The site now has an automated browser screenshot audit in `.github/workflows/visual-audit.yml` covering desktop and mobile views for Home, Tiles, Sanitary Ware, Showroom, and Contact.

## Current visual priorities

- Preserve the exact Tile Mart identity rather than redrawing the logo.
- Keep CTA contrast explicit and accessible.
- Improve mobile category-card visual weight.
- Avoid large decorative empty regions on narrow screens.
- Keep the palette focused on Tile Mart blue, warm stone, white, and dark navy.
- Prefer real showroom/product photography later when owner-supplied assets are available; do not present generated visuals as current stock.
