### Competitive observations

1. Competitors commonly position around large variety, quality, pricing, imported brands, and complete-home solutions.
2. Those claims are easy to copy but weak without evidence. Tile Mart should not imitate them with unsupported superlatives.
3. Tile Mart's strongest digital opportunity is a fast local site that makes product discovery and direct WhatsApp/showroom conversion extremely simple.
4. The website should be useful even before a complete product catalogue exists.
5. Local-search relevance should come from clear Peshawar/Karkhano context, structured data, focused category pages, accurate contact details, and a properly configured Google Business Profile—not keyword stuffing.
